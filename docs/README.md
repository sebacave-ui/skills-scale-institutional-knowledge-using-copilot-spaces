# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation space! This README provides an overview of the processes and documents used by our team to ensure consistent, reliable, and collaborative project delivery.

## Summary of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme manages projects using a repeatable, scalable lifecycle that emphasizes:

- **Clear roles and responsibilities** — Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, and QA teams work collaboratively with well-defined accountability
- **Defined milestones and deliverables** — Projects are broken into achievable phases with clear success criteria
- **Risk management and escalation paths** — Proactive identification and mitigation of project risks with clear escalation procedures
- **Iterative planning, execution, and improvement cycles** — Continuous refinement through regular retrospectives and feedback loops
- **Transparent communication and stakeholder alignment** — Regular updates and collaborative decision-making across all parties

Our approach ensures that projects stay on track, quality is maintained, and the entire team remains aligned on objectives and progress.
